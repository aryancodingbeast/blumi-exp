Noticed that while people want to declutter and refresh their wardrobes continuously, traditional listing platforms are high-friction and low-return. Decided to build a fun, swipe-to-match clothing exchange.

## Purpose

- **Who you help:** Gen Z and young fashion enthusiasts who want to refresh their style without spending a fortune or dealing with resale app lowball offers.
- **Problem you solve:** Fast fashion has made clothes cheap to buy but expensive to discard sustainably. Reselling clothes on traditional apps takes too much time (listing, haggling, shipping fees), while clothes sit unused in closets.
- **Desired transformation:** Users go from having an inactive closet full of unworn items to instantly swapping them for fresh styles through mutual matching, with zero friction and zero cost.
- **Why you:** Combining strong full-stack Next.js/Supabase development capability with an understanding of Gen Z fashion marketplace trends.

## Product

- **Name:** REVCLO
- **One-liner:** REVCLO helps young fashion enthusiasts exchange their clothes instantly through a Tinder-style swiping interface.
- **How it works:** Users list their items with at least 4 photos, sizes, and brand details. They browse other users' items in a swiping deck, selecting one of their own closet items to offer. If both users like each other's items, they match instantly. From there, they can confirm the exchange or choose to buy liked items directly.
- **Key capabilities:**
  - Catalog listing (requiring at least 4 images per item)
  - Interactive Tinder-like swiping deck (swipe left to pass, right to like)
  - Mutual closet item match detection & instant exchange creation
  - Shopping cart & direct purchase checkout flow
  - Matches dashboard & exchange status tracker
- **Platform:** web
- **Market differentiation:** Unlike standard resale marketplaces (Vinted, Depop) where users list, chat, negotiate, and pay, REVCLO enables instant clothes-for-clothes matching with zero-negotiation instant swaps.
- **Magic moment:** Swiping right on a vintage jacket, getting an instant "It's a Match!" celebration, and seeing that it was swapped for the jeans you listed and forgot about.

## Audience

- **Primary user:** Chloe, 20, college student with a closet full of clothes she wore once but doesn't want to sell for pennies. She wants new outfits for the weekend without spending her budget.
- **Secondary users:**
  - Eco-conscious thrifters who want sustainable circular fashion
  - Indie streetwear sellers who list unique curations to find trades
- **Current alternatives:** Depop, Vinted, Instagram DM trading, local thrift swap meets.
- **Frustrations:** Resale apps require extensive listing, lowball offers, shipping coordination, and high platform seller fees. Trading over DMs has no trust mechanism or structured matching.

## Business

- **Revenue model:** marketplace
- **90-day goal:** 1,000 active users in the campus community, 200 successful swaps completed, and positive feedback on the editorial styling.
- **6-month vision:** 10,000 active users, partnership with local vintage thrift boutiques, and premium swap tiers.
- **Constraints:** Solo developer building on Next.js, limited budget for server infrastructure.
- **Go-to-market:** Partner with university campus fashion clubs, launch on TikTok/Instagram Reels showing swap transformations, and host campus pop-up thrifting events.

## Tech Stack

- **App type:** web
- **Frontend:** Next.js (TypeScript) — SEO-friendly, server-side rendering for catalog, fast Vercel deployment.
- **Backend:** Supabase — instant Postgres database, authentication, and file storage for clothing photos.
- **Database:** Supabase PostgreSQL — native relationships for likes, catalog items, and matchmaking.
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth — easy social Google login and secure session management.
- **Payments:** Razorpay — integrated checkout flow for transaction fee processing.
- **Analytics:** None — skipped for early development
- **Email:** None — skipped for early development
- **Error tracking:** None — skipped for early development
